End to end

From first gun to final results

01

Course

Built from real marks, a laid shape or the course card — pushed to every phone.

02

Entries

Skippers enter from their phone; you see the live entry list.

03

Start

Arm the sequence; a synchronised countdown and horns on every device.

04

Finish

Tap sail numbers as boats cross — even offline — and send them to the scorer.

05

Results

From Sail Scoring or HalSail, shown when you choose to publish.

06

Protests & fees

Guided RRS protest forms; crew fees paid by card in the app.

This is the shape of the demo. Six stages of a race night, all in one place. Courses can be built three ways depending on how your club works. The start sequence is synchronised across every phone with horns on the second. Finishes are captured offline-first and sent straight to the scorer. Results come from Sail Scoring or HalSail and stay hidden until you publish. Protests follow the RRS forms, and crew can pay by card.
