A race officer’s evening

Course

Anaylysing the weather, communicating Via Whatsapp, VHF, Noticeboard etc..

Paper finish sheets

Written on a damp clipboard, then re-typed into scoring later.

Chasing fees

Who sailed, who paid, and who owes — keeping it fair and accountable

The noticeboard

Results and protests pinned up for whoever passes by.

Most of a race officer’s evening goes on paperwork and relaying information — not on running a fair race.

Get a show of hands: who has re-typed a finish sheet at 11pm? Who has read a course out over VHF three times? The point is that race officers spend a lot of the evening relaying and transcribing information. RaceOps is about taking that load off.
