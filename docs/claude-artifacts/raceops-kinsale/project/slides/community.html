For the sailing community

Easier to get out on the water

Everyone informed

Course, start time, wind and tide on every phone before leaving the pontoon.

Crew meet boats

Boats looking for crew and sailors looking for a boat find each other.

A way in for newcomers

A clear path from a first outing as a visitor to full membership.

Fair and open

Results and protests handled in the open, using the RRS forms.

Fees without fuss

Crew pay by card or phone — no cash tin, no chasing.

Clubs share the work

One platform, so a fix for one club is a fix for all.

The bigger picture. Racing depends on volunteers and on crew turning up. Anything that makes it easier to know what is happening, to find a boat, to get from a first sail to membership, and to pay without hassle helps keep fleets healthy. And because the clubs share one platform, the effort is shared too.
